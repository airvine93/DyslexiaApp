# Five prompt varieties

Five written prompts for the look of a video. Each is also built into the script, so `node render.mjs render varieties --word 12` renders idea 12 in all five.
Made by `node render.mjs manifest` from `VARIETIES` in `src/design.js`. `{word}`, `{meaning}` and `{lesson}` stand for the idea's own text.

## V1. Canopy light

Background: `dapple`. Settings: theme navy, accent butter, hard faint, reveal morph, font lexend, feat font, pace medium, cam drift.

> Three.js, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, every frame a pure function of time. Deep navy background (#0E1726). Behind the text, soft patches of warm light slide and breathe slowly, like sun coming through a canopy; never brighter than a quarter of the text. Camera drifts a few pixels. Text is built from one textured plane per letter. Open with "{word}" in large pale-yellow letters arriving from depth, then "{meaning}" as a small white line and a large yellow line. Shrink these to a title and fade in "{lesson}" in plain white sans-serif. After it has been read, let the lesson sink into small, faint grey serif text and ask "Find this difficult to read?". Then every letter glides to its new place while its shape cross-fades into Lexend with wider spacing. Hold the easy version for at least five seconds. Close on one line and an "Easy Reader" pill.

## V2. Falling leaves

Background: `leaves`. Settings: theme ink, accent mint, hard swim, reveal scatter, font opendyslexic, feat bionic, pace medium, cam still.

> Three.js, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, every frame a pure function of time. Near-black background (#111114). About thirty simple leaf shapes in mint and pale gold fall slowly through the depth of the frame, each turning on all three axes, most of them out of the way of the text. Locked camera. One textured plane per letter. Open with "{word}" in large mint letters, then "{meaning}". Shrink to a title and show "{lesson}" in white. Then let the letters of the lesson drift a little out of place, each on its own slow wobble, and ask "Does reading ever feel like this?". For the change, the letters lift off the page and scatter into the air like the leaves, then settle back into OpenDyslexic. Next, make the first part of every word bold. Hold, then close on one line and an "Easy Reader" pill.

## V3. Sun shafts

Background: `shafts`. Settings: theme navy, accent peach, hard cramped, reveal sweep, font atkinson, feat ruler, pace slow, cam push.

> Three.js, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, every frame a pure function of time. Deep navy background. Broad slanting shafts of peach-gold light fall from the top right and fade before the bottom, shifting very slowly, with fine dust turning in them at different depths. Camera pushes in by about five percent over the whole video. One textured plane per letter. Open with "{word}" in large peach letters, then "{meaning}". Shrink to a title and show "{lesson}" in white. Then squeeze the lesson into small serif text with tight lines and tight letters and ask "Hard to read?". A thin bar of light sweeps down the page; each line changes to Atkinson Hyperlegible as the bar crosses it. Then a soft highlighted band, a reading ruler, steps down one line at a time while the other lines dim. Slow pace throughout. Close on one line and an "Easy Reader" pill.

## V4. Page in the shade

Background: `shade`. Settings: theme cream, accent apricot, hard glare, reveal wave, font andika, feat tint, pace medium, cam still.

> Three.js, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, every frame a pure function of time. A warm cream page (#F6EFE0) with dark ink (#1E2430). The soft shadows of leaves move across the page, as if it were being read outdoors under a tree. Locked camera. One textured plane per letter. Open with "{word}" in large deep-apricot letters, then "{meaning}". Shrink to a title and show "{lesson}" in dark ink. Then the shade lifts: the page flares to hard white and the lesson shrinks to thin black serif text, and the video asks "Find this difficult to read?". For the change, a wave rolls across the lines from left to right, lifting each letter toward the camera as it becomes Andika. Then a soft apricot colour overlay wipes across the whole frame and stays. Close on one line and an "Easy Reader" pill.

## V5. Soft focus

Background: `bokeh`. Settings: theme dusk, accent sky, hard crowd, reveal flip, font readex, feat all, pace fast, cam drift.

> Three.js, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, every frame a pure function of time. Deep plum background (#241631). Large out-of-focus discs of light in pale blue and gold float upward at different depths and slowly brighten and dim, with a slightly brighter rim like a camera lens blur. Camera drifts gently. One textured plane per letter. Quick pace. Open with "{word}" in large pale-blue letters, then "{meaning}". Shrink to a title and show "{lesson}" in white. Then pull the letters of the lesson together until they touch and ask "Do the words crowd together?". For the change, each letter flips over in turn, like a card, and comes up in Readex Pro. Then show three features one after another, each with its name above the text: bold word starts, a colour overlay, a reading ruler. Close on one line and an "Easy Reader" pill.
